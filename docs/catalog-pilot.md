## One proposed pilot

Choose **one MCPize fixed monthly subscription with an included hard allowance, no paid overages and one externally hosted customer instance**, conditional on owner approval and the checks below. The customer supplies and controls its Clarivate application key. The subscription pays for managed deployment and support. The customer’s existing WoS entitlement covers its upstream use. Use the bridge’s existing Python deployment and keep gateway integration at the deployment boundary. MCPize’s existing-endpoint route is the shorter integration if it can restrict paid access to the approved customer and authenticate to the origin. Pause paid launch if those conditions fail. The owner can revisit the same single pilot with the keyless `literature` profile if WoS rights are unavailable and the literature use is approved. [Hosted credentials ADR](decisions/0002-hosted-provider-credentials.md) · [Credential and rights decision #2](https://github.com/hrabosh/wos-mcp-bridge/issues/2)

The owner sets the price, allowance and spend cap after checking the signed provider terms and actual costs. Budget the marketplace fee, hosting and support separately from upstream spend. Written permission must cover the host and gateway, key provisioning and rotation, named users, the AI client, allowed fields, cache and retention. Disable provider caching until permissions cover it. An operator-owned WoS key would require written commercial rights and aggregate and per-customer quota controls.

### Pilot acceptance and withdrawal

- [ ] Confirm restricted customer access, gateway-to-origin authentication and direct-origin access controls. Admit only the approved users. Keep the provider key, client pools, cache and limiters in the dedicated process. Verify rotation and revocation using synthetic credentials, and verify that credentials, queries and records stay out of gateway and origin logs.
- [ ] Test discovery, authentication and one permitted successful call through the intended MCP client and exact deployment. Test missing, expired and invalid credentials and a second account. Record transport, client, deployment revision and tool schemas.
- [ ] Settle the counting policy before sale. The proposed allowance counts one successful tool result per logical call, including a permitted cache hit. Discovery, auth failures, upstream errors, limit rejections and cancellation before completion consume zero allowance. The upstream may already have consumed quota when a response fails or is cancelled. Record the gateway’s actual behavior, including a lost response after completion, which needs an explicit rule. If the gateway cannot meet this policy, use a verified, owner-approved policy disclosed before purchase or pause the pilot.
- [ ] Use a verified non-production test mode to check a cold success, warm cache hit, upstream timeout/error and 429, subscriber limit exhaustion and reset, cancellation before and after upstream dispatch, disconnect after completion, gateway redelivery and a deliberate repeat. Compare gateway usage and charges with origin attempts. Verify that a retried delivery of the same logical call cannot generate two charges, or record the limitation and require owner acceptance before launch. Confirm that reaching the cap stops additional billable work.
- [ ] Before launch, rehearse a rollback using a known-good release and verify the listing and auth from the client. If withdrawing, stop new subscriptions and resolve current ones under the agreed terms. Disable the gateway route, revoke credentials, remove stored material under the approved retention policy, and communicate any endpoint change to authorized users.
